How it decides

Every road gets a score.
Biggest score gets green.

Score = traffic waiting × time waited. A bus counts 3, a car 1, a bike 0.3. One real moment:

8 bikes, 2 cars, 1 bus. Wins. Green.

four rules no score can break

Nobody waits past 90 s. Hit the cap, you're next. Period.

Ambulance beats everything. But only for a bounded time.

No flicker, no hogging. Green lasts 5 s minimum, 45 s maximum.

Queue gone? Move on. Green ends early when its lane runs empty.

R.E.L.A.Y.10
The honest slide

Pros and cons. Yes, we wrote the cons down.

works for us

✓ 50 to 300× cheaper. The camera is already there.

✓ Serves vehicles, pedestrians and ambulances. Nobody waits forever.

✓ Every claim measured in side-by-side tests, not promised.

✓ Worst case: it becomes today's timer. The floor is the status quo.

works against us, and our answer

✕ Cameras have bad nights. Answer: train on them. If vision fails, fall back to fixed timing.

✕ Each site needs a tune-up day. Answer: the labelling is automatic. A day, not a project.

✕ Total gridlock beats every signal plan. Answer: we win everywhere below it.

✕ KTM's lights died of neglect, not tech. Answer: commodity parts, remote checks, trust earned in shadow mode.
