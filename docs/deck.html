R.E.L.A.Y.10
The honest slide

Pros and cons.
Yes, we wrote the cons down.

for

✓ 50 to 300× cheaper. Camera already there.

✓ Vehicles, pedestrians, ambulances. All served.

✓ Measured, not promised.

✓ Worst case = today's timer.

against, with our answer

✕ Cameras have bad nights. Train on them; else fall back to timer.

✕ Each site needs a tune-up day. Automatic. A day, not a project.

✕ Total gridlock beats everyone. We win everywhere below it.

✕ Kathmandu's lights died of neglect. Ordinary parts, remote checks, shadow mode.
