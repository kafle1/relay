The problem

Kathmandu's junctions run on blind timers —
or on a police officer's arm

  • ▸Fixed timers ignore reality. A lane holding 2 motorbikes gets the same green as a lane holding 40 — while everyone else waits up to 200 s.
  • ▸Most installed lights are dead. The city's signals have sat dark for years; traffic police direct flows by hand at peak hours.
  • ▸Ambulances get no priority. There is no mechanism — sirens sit in the same queue as everything else.
  • ▸Commercial adaptive systems cost $20k–80k per intersection (SCATS, SCOOT, Surtrac) — priced for cities that are not ours.
a real cycle at a fixed-timer junction
loaded lane — waits 200 s
empty lane — full green anyway

Green time is the scarcest resource at a junction. Fixed timers spend it without looking.

The system

Camera → counts → decision → lights. A closed loop.

SeeOne CCTV camera per junction. YOLO detects every vehicle class — bikes, cars, buses, trucks, ambulances.
→
WeighPer-approach counts, PCU-weighted (a bus ≠ a motorbike). Smoothed against detector flicker.
→
DecideMax-pressure control: serve the heaviest demand, skip empty approaches, age waiting lanes so nobody starves.
→
ActuateSignal heads follow the decision — with yellow + all-red clearance, minimum greens, emergency preemption.

Empty-skip

An approach with nothing on it never gets a green. The core waste, gone by construction.

Ambulance preemption

Detected visually and by transponder; its arm goes green, bounded so the junction can never be held hostage.

Pedestrian fairness

Waiting pedestrians build pressure too — bounded walk windows, held while anyone is on the zebra.

relay07
Under the hood

Small, inspectable, hard to kill

Detector

YOLO11s fine-tuned per site on auto-labelled frames — 0.95 mAP@50 on our junctions. Stock weights for unseen cameras, tuned weights after a day on site.

Controller

~400 lines of max-pressure Python. Every safety property is an executable assertion: no conflicting greens, bounded waits, bounded preemption, clearance always.

Degrades safely

Camera drops → last counts age out → falls back to fixed cycling. Bad frames, fog, night: the controller never sees raw pixels, only smoothed counts.

Edge cases as a register

85 documented cases — dropped RTSP, stuck detections, phantom boxes, saturated gridlock, dual ambulances on crossing axes — each handled in code.

Runs on anything

Laptop-class inference at 27 ms/frame. A $250 edge box handles a junction; no cloud, no connectivity requirement.

Honest A/B

Every headline number comes from seeded, identical-arrival comparisons — the demo shows both panels live, and the verdict is computed from what's on screen.

Beyond one junction

Coordination is software, not hardware

three junctions, green-wave: an ambulance's corridor clears ahead of it, junction by junction

Junctions share their queue state. A downstream jam lowers the score of feeding it; an ambulance triggers a sequential green wave along its route.
